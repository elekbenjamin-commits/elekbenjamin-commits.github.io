Personal backup utility

Rclone Backup

Rclone Backup is a personal-use configuration of the open-source rclone tool. It creates a backup of the account owner's Google Drive files to a locally connected external storage device.

Google Drive access is requested in read-only mode. Google Docs, Sheets and Slides may be exported to standard office file formats for recoverable local backups.

This is not a public service. It does not offer accounts or access to other people's files, and this website does not receive Google Drive content.
